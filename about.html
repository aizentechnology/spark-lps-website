About Us

Our Organization

Spark positions itself as one of the pioneers in the domain of learning and development, offering end to end solutions. The company provides comprehensive services spanning corporate readiness, career planning, and customized learning frameworks.

Our tagline: Empowering people for enhanced productivity. We deliver 360-degree solutions across four main service categories — corporate training, educational institution skilling, vocational training, and global employability development.

Our Vision

To be the most revered and sought after partner for learning and performance solutions to enable sustainable transformation.

Our Mission
